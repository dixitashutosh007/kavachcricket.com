Signature Annual Family Evening

KAvach Timeout

Beyond intense weekend cricket matches, KAvach is a deeply connected family. Every year, we host KAvach Timeout — an electric, fun-filled sports evening where player spouses, children, and members unite for a multi-sport championship followed by a celebratory family dinner!

Annual Family Festival Players, Spouses & Kids Competing Together

Two Teams Led by Families Battle for the Timeout Trophy

During KAvach Timeout, the entire club is divided into two dynamic family-led teams. Spouses, toddlers, teenagers, and seasoned cricketers join hands, score points across multi-sport challenges, and fight for glory to lift the coveted KAvach Timeout Trophy!

The Championship Arena

6 Competitive Family Sports

Every game is designed to be inclusive, high-energy, and accessible to family members of all age groups.

Mini Cricket

Fast-paced, high-voltage backyard-style cricket with modified soft-ball rules. Spouses hit boundaries, kids bowl decisive overs, and cricketers play as supporting anchors!

Football (Futsal)

A 5-a-side arena football clash filled with swift passing, teamwork, spectacular goalkeeping saves, and plenty of laughs across the pitch.

Dodge Ball

Adrenaline-fueled dodging, diving, and lightning-fast throws. One of the most popular crowd favorites where youngsters and adults test their agility.

Kho Kho

The legendary traditional Indian tag sport that brings pure childhood nostalgia. Fast sprints, clever dummy turns, and tactical chasing that electrifies the ground.

Pitthu (Lagori / 7 Stones)

Targeting the 7-stone tower with precision, scrambling to rebuild it, and dodging the ball! A timeless street classic where whole families strategize together.

Tug of War

The grand finale showdown of sheer strength and synchronized team pulling power! Player families line up behind the rope with roaring cheers from the sidelines.

Grand Finale

Awards Ceremony & Family Dinner

After the sweat, cheering, and thrilling finishes on the sports field, the evening transitions to an unforgettable celebration banquet.

  • The Timeout Trophy: Awarded to the victorious family-led squad
  • Medals for Kids & Spouses: Commemorating sportsmanship and participation
  • Delicious Family Dinner: A relaxed social evening of food, music, and bonding
