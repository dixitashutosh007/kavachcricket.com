Tournament Pillars

What Makes KPL Extraordinary

KPL combines professional tournament production with the warmth and camaraderie of our corporate cricket family.

Grand Auction Night

Every registered KAvach player is entered into an auction pool with base price ratings. Franchise team owners bid with virtual purse points to build balanced squads across batting, pace, spin, and all-round departments.

Colored Kits & Branding

Each franchise wears bespoke colored jerseys featuring custom crests, names, and numbers. High-grade breathable athletic fabrics ensure players look and feel like international athletes.

Live Digital Streaming

Every match is broadcast live with multiple camera angles, digital on-screen graphics, ball-by-ball CricClubs scoring, and engaging commentary so your colleagues, friends, and family can watch your highlights.

Orange & Purple Caps

Individual excellence is recognized with official awards: Orange Cap for highest run-getter, Purple Cap for top wicket-taker, MVP of the Tournament, Best Fielder, and Maximum Sixes awards.

Top Turf Stadiums

Matches are contested on manicured turf pitches across Bangalore equipped with sightscreens, 65+ meter boundary ropes, white balls, and KSCA-certified panel umpires.

Annual Gala Banquet

KPL culminates in a grand awards presentation banquet with players, families, team owners, and sponsors. A memorable celebration of sportsmanship, dedication, and club brotherhood.

Participation

How to Qualify for the KPL Auction

Participation in KPL is exclusively open to active, registered members of KAvach Cricket Club. Members who regularly participate in our weekday morning practice sessions at Play Arena or our weekend leather-ball matches are drafted into the official auction catalog.
