Tournament Pillars

What Makes KPL Extraordinary

KPL combines professional tournament production with the warmth and camaraderie of our corporate cricket family.

IPL Style Auction Based Squad Selection

Every registered KAvach player enters the auction catalog with base price tiers. Franchise team owners strategize and bid with virtual purse points to assemble balanced, competitive tournament squads.

Award Nights for Players

KPL culminates in an extraordinary awards presentation night and banquet with players, families, and owners. Celebrating sportsmanship, individual heroics, and championship glory together.

Commentary & Live Streaming

Every league clash is streamed live with multiple camera views, dynamic on-screen CricClubs scorecards, and high-energy live commentary so families and friends never miss a moment.

Professional Video and Photography

Dedicated match-day media teams capture high-definition photos, slow-motion highlight reels, boundary celebrations, and wicket cuts for every player to cherish lifelong memories.

Orange Cap, Purple Cap, MVP & Championship Trophy

Prestigious individual honors await top performers: the Orange Cap for leading run-scorer, Purple Cap for highest wicket-taker, MVP of the Tournament, and the ultimate Championship Trophy.

Bespoke Colored Kits & Team Branding

Each franchise squad takes the field in customized colored jerseys with crests, player names, and squad numbers, bringing true franchise cricket atmosphere to curated Bangalore grounds.

Participation

How to Qualify for the KPL Auction

Participation in KPL is exclusively open to active, registered members of KAvach Cricket Club. Members who regularly participate in our weekday morning practice sessions at Play Arena or our weekend leather-ball matches are drafted into the official auction catalog.

If you are a working professional in Bangalore looking to experience the rush of tournament cricket, book your trial session today to get started!
