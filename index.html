Our Story

From 4 Strangers on a Pitch to a 350+ Strong Family

KAvach Cricket Club began in early 2022 with a simple, heartfelt idea: to play 45 minutes of morning cricket for health, fitness, and love of the game. Four complete strangers—working professionals living in South Bangalore, returning to leather-ball cricket after a 15-year hiatus—connected through an open morning net invite. To their surprise and delight, over 15 passionate players turned up on morning one!

On 5th February 2022, KAvach played its very first official match. Today, that small gathering has grown into one of Bangalore's most respected cricketing associations: ranked among India's Top 20 and Bangalore's Top 5 amateur cricket clubs with over 900+ matches played.

Commitment & Integrity

Club Culture & Code of Conduct

KAvach is built on mutual respect, shared accountability, and the true spirit of cricket.

Safety & Protective Gear

An abdominal guard is mandatory for all batting drills without exception. Hard-shell helmets, pads, and gloves are strictly enforced when facing leather balls in both match and net conditions.

The Spirit of Cricket

Zero tolerance for abusive language, umpire dissent, or unsportsmanlike behavior. Indiscipline or misconduct leads to immediate eviction without any refund.

Fair Match Opportunities

Match selections are coordinated by our Think Tank based on net attendance, form, team balance, and positive sporting attitude, ensuring transparent and fair opportunities for all players.
