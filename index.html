KAvach Studio

High-Performance Cricket Training Facility

Located inside Play Arena, Sarjapur Road, KAvach Studio is our dedicated weekday morning cricket training center (Tuesday to Friday, 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM). Designed specifically for focused skill acquisition, technical conditioning, and match preparation before your workday begins:

  • Automated Bowling Machine Drills: Practice against variable high-speed seam, swing, and spin with pinpoint length control to master frontfoot drives, pull shots, and backfoot defense.
  • Sidearm Throwdowns & Turf Nets: Intense batting against genuine match pace and bouncer simulations in covered turf-matting nets with quality leather balls.
  • Slip Catching & Fielding Agility: High-repetition reflex catching cradles, boundary dive drills, direct hits, and dynamic ground fielding.
  • Video Stroke Analysis & Peer Coaching: Stance calibration, bat-swing mechanics review, and tactical feedback from experienced club captains and KSCA players.

Our Story

From 4 to 400

KAvach Cricket Club began in early 2022 with a simple, heartfelt idea: to play 45 minutes of morning cricket for health, fitness, and love of the game. Four complete strangers—working professionals living in South Bangalore, returning to leather-ball cricket after a 15-year hiatus—connected through an open morning net invite. To their surprise and delight, over 15 passionate players turned up on morning one!

On 5th February 2022, KAvach played its very first official match. Today, that small gathering has grown into one of Bangalore's most respected cricketing associations: ranked among India's Top 20 and Bangalore's Top 5 amateur cricket clubs with over 900+ matches played.

Commitment & Integrity

Club Culture & Code of Conduct

KAvach is built on mutual respect, shared accountability, and the true spirit of cricket.

Safety & Protective Gear

An abdominal guard is mandatory for all batting drills without exception. Hard-shell helmets, pads, and gloves are strictly enforced when facing leather balls in both match and net conditions.

The Spirit of Cricket

Zero tolerance for abusive language, umpire dissent, or unsportsmanlike behavior. Indiscipline or misconduct leads to immediate eviction without any refund.

Fair Match Opportunities

Match selections are coordinated by our Think Tank based on net attendance, form, team balance, and positive sporting attitude, ensuring transparent and fair opportunities for all players.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining KAvach Cricket Club, practice sessions, player profiles, and eligibility.

Joining KAvach Cricket Club is simple and structured around your goals and weekly schedule:

  1. Join Weekdays Practice Sessions (KAvach Studio): If you want regular morning training and skill development, book a trial net session (Tue–Fri, 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM at Play Arena, Sarjapur Road) by filling our Trial Booking Form below.
  2. Join Weekend Club Matches: If you are match-ready and want to play competitive Saturday/Sunday 20 or 30-over leather ball matches, complete our Official Registration Form ↗. Share your cricketing background or CricHeroes profile link.

Once submitted, our Think Tank committee will review your profile and connect with you on WhatsApp to confirm your trial slot or match assessment.

KAvach Cricket Club is ranked among Bangalore's Top 5 amateur cricket clubs and India's Top 20. Designed specifically for working professionals, adults, and senior cricketers, KAvach provides weekday morning practice sessions (7:00 AM – 9:00 AM) at Play Arena, Sarjapur Road in Bangalore South, structured 20/30-over weekend leather ball matches, and the marquee KAvach Premier League (KPL).

KAvach plays 100% seasoned leather ball cricket (red and white ball). We do not operate tennis ball teams. We warmly welcome senior and veteran cricketers (in their 40s and 50s) returning to cricket after years in corporate life, as well as young adults (16+). Our coordinators match players to suitable squads to ensure balanced, safe, and competitive games.

Our 400+ community is predominantly composed of working professionals from across Bangalore: Software Architects and IT engineers (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Infosys), Doctors & Surgeons, Civil Servants & Government Officers, Startup Founders, CEOs, and corporate executives, alongside talented university cricketers and KSCA Division/State-level players.

KAvach welcomes players aged 16 years and onwards. We accommodate young aspiring players, busy working professionals in their 20s, 30s, and 40s, right through to veteran players returning for their second cricketing innings.

Because we play exclusively with seasoned leather balls, players must possess foundational cricketing skills, coachability, physical readiness, and a team-first attitude. As one of Bangalore's top-ranked clubs, we are selective about who joins our community to preserve our club culture and ground safety. Trial sessions allow our Think Tank to evaluate skill alignment and commitment. All rights of admission and membership are strictly reserved by club management.
